# About Me

I am a **Ph.D. student** in Information and Communication Engineering at the [University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)](https://www.ustc.edu.cn/), advised by **Prof. Li Xiao**. I began my graduate studies at USTC in 2024 and entered the Ph.D. program in September 2026. Before that, I received my B.E. in Communication Engineering from [Anhui University](https://www.ahu.edu.cn/) in 2024, ranking **1st in my major**.

My research focuses on **reinforcement learning for LLM agents**, **agent memory**, and **continual personalization**. I also work on medical AI, including multi-turn medical dialogue, brain network modeling, and medical image analysis. Since June 2025, I have been a research intern at **Alibaba Quark**, working on medical LLMs and agent post-training.

### Research Interests

- **Agentic Reinforcement Learning** — Efficient training and exploration for multi-turn, interactive LLM agents.
- **Agent Memory & Personalization** — Improving memory use, cross-session learning, and adaptation to individual preferences.
- **Medical AI** — Intelligent medical dialogue and graph learning for neuroimaging and clinical prediction.

I am happy to discuss research and potential collaborations. Feel free to [email me](mailto:ustc_crk@mail.ustc.edu.cn)!
